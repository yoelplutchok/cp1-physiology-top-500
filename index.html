Two pumps in series: circuit layout and flow

1Asked 1×
How are the right and left hearts arranged in the circulation?
  1. A. In parallel, each side feeding its own loop
  2. B. In series, in one continuous loop
  3. C. In series, but each circuit a closed loop of its own
AnswerB. In series, in one continuous loop
WhyEverything the right heart ejects crosses the lungs to the left heart, and everything the left ejects returns to the right: two pumps in series, one loop.

  2. B. Q = ΔP × R
  3. C. Q = R / ΔP
